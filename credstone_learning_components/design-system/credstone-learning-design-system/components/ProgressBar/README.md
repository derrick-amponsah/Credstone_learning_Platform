A horizontal bar that shows how far through a course or lesson a learner is. The fill is `progress-fill` on a `track`; on a `navy` ground wrap it in `cs-on-navy` and the fill becomes `gold`.

The consumer provides the percentage as the fill width and as the visible value beside the bar. Always show the number: the bar alone is not enough to read progress. Keep it 8 to 12px tall.

Do not use a progress bar for anything that is not progress, such as a score or a deadline.
