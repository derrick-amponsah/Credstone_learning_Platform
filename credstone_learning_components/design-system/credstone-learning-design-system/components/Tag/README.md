A short uppercase label that tells a learner the status or level of a course. Use `cs-tag--required` for mandatory training with its due date, `cs-tag--level` for Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced, `cs-tag--done` for completed items and `cs-tag--navy` for certificate tracks.

The consumer provides the words. Every tag carries a word, never colour alone. Put one tag at the top of a course card, left-aligned. Keep the text under four words.

Do not use the Required tag for anything that is not mandatory, and do not stack more than one tag on a card.
