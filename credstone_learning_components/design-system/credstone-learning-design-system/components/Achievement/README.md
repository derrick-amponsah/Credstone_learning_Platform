Streak and badge tiles that reward progress. Use `cs-stat` for a number with a label (streak, badge count), `cs-badge` with a `cs-medal` gold disc for an earned badge, and `cs-badge--locked` with `cs-medal--locked` and a lock icon for one still to unlock.

The consumer provides a short name, one line saying how it was earned or what unlocks it, and a 24 to 28px stroke icon. Locked badges always say how many days or lessons remain. Show at most six badges at a time and lead with earned ones.

Do not use streaks for required training, and do not shame a missed day: when a streak ends, say "Start a new streak today."
